About

A one-person studio

CMounier Consulting is a French SASU founded by Clément Mounier, a lifelong video game enthusiast. The studio creates its games from start to finish: game design, development, graphics integration and publishing on the App Store.

Being small means we can take care of every detail and listen closely to players' feedback.
